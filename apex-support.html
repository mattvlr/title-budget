Apex Drive Log — Support

iPhone drive tracker. Records your route, times the roads you repeat, and tracks what the fuel costs.

Need help? Email bookmonger990@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong. One person answers these, usually within a few days.

Common questions

My drive stopped recording when I locked my phone

Apex needs the Always location permission to keep recording with the screen off. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Apex and choose Always. Apex only gets one chance to ask for that, so if you said no the first time it has to be changed here.

Also check that Precise Location is on in the same screen. Without it iOS gives Apex a rough area instead of a position, and the trace will be useless.

Does Apex record when I am not driving?

No. It records between the moment you tap Start and the moment you tap Stop, and at no other time. While it is recording, iOS shows the blue location indicator in your status bar. If you do not see that indicator, Apex is not using your location.

Where are my drives stored? Can I get them back?

In a database file inside the app on your iPhone. There is no cloud backup — that is deliberate, and it means deleting the app deletes the drives. Export any drive you want to keep as a GPX file from its detail screen.

No segments have appeared

Apex only creates a segment when it finds at least a quarter of a mile covered in at least 30 seconds along one named street. Short trips and routes that turn constantly will not produce one. Drive the same stretch of road a second time and you will get a leaderboard with two entries on it.

The leaderboard only has me on it

That is how it works. Apex ranks you against your own previous runs of a segment. There is no leaderboard shared with other people.

Photographing the pump does nothing

That feature needs your own OpenAI API key, which you create at platform.openai.com/api-keys and paste into Settings → OpenAI. Tap “Test key” there to check it. A key with no billing set up on the OpenAI account will return an error. Each photo costs about a tenth of a cent, billed by OpenAI to you.

You do not need it. Every fillup can be typed in by hand and everything else in the fuel section works the same either way.

My MPG looks wrong

Real MPG is calculated from the difference between odometer readings on consecutive fillups, so it needs an odometer reading on each one and it needs you to fill the tank each time. Miss a fillup and the next figure will be too high.

Does it work on iPad, Apple Watch or CarPlay?

No. iPhone only, portrait only.

Battery

Recording a drive keeps the GPS at navigation accuracy, which uses real power — expect roughly what a turn-by-turn navigation app uses. Keep the phone on a charger for long drives. Apex does not use the GPS at all when a drive is not recording.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: your drives stay on your iPhone, there is no account, there is no analytics, and the only optional thing that leaves the device is a fuel receipt photo if you have set up your own OpenAI key.

Reporting a bug

Email the address above with: what you expected, what happened, your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the Version and Build numbers from Apex's Settings → About. If it involves a specific drive, say roughly when you recorded it.
