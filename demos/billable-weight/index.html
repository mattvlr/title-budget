How it was checked

  • 91 assertions across 8 box scenarios.
  • USPS moved its divisor from 166 to 139 on 12 July 2026, roughly 19% more billable weight. Anything still dividing by 166 understates the cost.
  • Each side rounds up to the next whole inch before the volume is worked out. The tests use a box where rounding sides first and rounding the volume after give different answers (5 lb vs 4 lb), so they prove which method the tool uses.
