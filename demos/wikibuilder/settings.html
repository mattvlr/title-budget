When enabled, each generated section receives an AI-crafted image using the article text for context.

Cost warning. Illustrations are the most expensive part of a generation. Each article adds one extra text call and one image render per section — typically 6–10 of each. Image renders are billed per image and usually dominate the total, so a single illustrated article can cost many times an unillustrated one. Leave this off while you are evaluating, and check current OpenAI pricing before enabling it.
