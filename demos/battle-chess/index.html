What you should know before buying

  • No support. One-time download, sold as-is, no bug fixes and no updates.
  • Single player against an AI. No multiplayer, no accounts, no matchmaking.
  • File-backed state, one worker. Fine for playing; not a service you can scale horizontally without replacing the store.
  • Balance is not tuned. scripts/simulate.py runs AI-vs-AI batches so you can measure it yourself, but nobody has spent weeks balancing this.
  • No art of its own. Pieces are the standard chessboard.js set; there are no sprites, no sound and no animation beyond piece movement.
  • One licensing question is open, and it is not the code. The chess rules are written in-repo and carry no third-party licence, but the 12 vendored piece images derive from Cburnett's Wikimedia Commons set, commonly distributed under the share-alike CC BY-SA 3.0. Which licence applies to those exact files is not confirmed. They are swappable through chessboard.js's pieceTheme option and nothing else depends on them. The licence states this in full.
  • The army roster panel is not shown here. Its HP bars would not paint in the headless browser used for these captures and I could not confirm how they look in a real one, so it is left out rather than shown possibly wrong.
