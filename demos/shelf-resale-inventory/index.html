How it was checked

  • 600 tests pass (python -m pytest -q, 48 seconds) and ruff check . is clean.
  • All 36 pages load. Every parameterless GET route was enumerated from Flask's own app.url_map — not a hand-written list — requested, and returned 200, with zero severe console errors.
  • 9 Alembic migrations apply from an empty database, and the seeder writes two fictional years of trading in one command.
  • Verified twice: once on the source tree, then again from inside the packaged zip in a clean virtualenv.

Not included

  • No authentication of any kind. It is meant to be bound to 127.0.0.1 on a machine you control. Anyone who can reach the port can read and change everything.
  • No eBay or Etsy API integration. Sales arrive by CSV import or manual entry. Nothing syncs.
  • No sold-comps lookup. The design document records it as a deliberate v1 exclusion.
  • No refund entry screen and no item delete route — both stated on the listing because you will find them.
  • Bookkeeping output, not tax advice.
