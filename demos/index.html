Twelve Black-and-White Conversions

Pulling saturation to zero gives you one black-and-white image and no control. A darkroom contrast filter is a weighted mix of all three channels — which a 1D curve cannot do and a 3D LUT is built for. Twelve documented weightings, mixed in linear light. A red flower and a green leaf convert to 100 against 99 under Neutral, the same grey; 174 against 68 under the red filter. No film stock is named anywhere.
