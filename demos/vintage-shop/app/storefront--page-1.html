About Rosewater & Rust

Rosewater & Rust is a two-room vintage shop in Cedar Hollow. We buy whole estates, sort them on the back bench, and keep the pieces that still have some life in them. Furniture gets cleaned and waxed, audio gear gets bench-tested, and every garment is measured with a tape rather than trusted to a size label.
