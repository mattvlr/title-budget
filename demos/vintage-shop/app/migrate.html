What this wizard does

Use this when you're moving your shop between installs — for example, standing up a fresh Heroku app, pointing the database somewhere new, or restoring after a backup.

  1. Drop in the files you exported from your old shop (items CSV and/or photos ZIP).
  2. The wizard will tell you exactly what it's about to do before touching anything.
  3. If it looks right, you click Apply. If it doesn't, you click Cancel, and nothing is changed.
